## 1. Background & objectives
The engine is a beachhead-first two-sided marketplace sizing-and-validation system for preMercado (NL, launch 1 Sep 2026). It drives four decisions: **ignite** (open a market), **rescue** (inject effort into an underperforming one), **abort** (stop), **expand** (move to adjacent/national geography). To drive them it must estimate, per segment and horizon: the campaign-response registration rate ρ on each side, the transportable type-rate B_type, between-postcode heterogeneity τ², and the two-sided ignition frontier ρ_lower. In scope: the inferential and decision machinery. Out of scope: architecture, GTM, UX, regulatory, pricing-as-commercial-decision.

## 2. Estimands (the spine)

**2.1 The three-rung geographic ladder.** For the launch segment's per-household registration rate, define three nested estimands:
- **B_founder** — the rate in the two specific founder PC4s (1017, 2584). Carries (i) a non-replicable *founder-presence uplift* and (ii) *selection-on-outcome* (the postcodes were chosen partly for expected success → winner's curse). These two components are **aliased and not separately identifiable** post-hoc; only their sum δ is estimable, and only against controls. The SBC (§7.2) confirms this quantitatively: δ is non-recoverable (rank-mean 155/999) when selection is unmodelled.
- **B_type** — the rate in the matched postcode *type* where the founders are unknown. **This is the quantity that transports to expansion.**
- **A** — the national rate.
Nesting: B_founder is a biased estimate of B_type ⊂ A. Early founder-PC4 data point-identifies only B_founder. The harness (module 1) shows that treating B_founder as B_type imports an upward bias of roughly **3.9× under the working inputs** — so the ladder is not pedantry, it is the difference between a transportable number and a 4×-inflated one.

**2.2 Full estimand index.** Each rate/size is indexed by {rung B_founder | B_type | A} × {segment} × {side: listing | seeking} × {horizon: 30 | 90 | 180 d}. Primary at launch: B_founder and B_type for the launch segment, both sides, t90. Secondary: A (expansion), other segments, t30/t180.

**2.3 Conditioning of ρ (channel-conditional, live taxonomy).** ρ is a *campaign-response rate among households/users actually reached, per channel* — stimulus-conditional and denominator-conditional. The engine carries a first-class `channels` dimension (`figures.channel_id`); the NL set verified live (8 channels) is: the digital-primary buys **`meta`, `google_search`, `iamexpat`** (tier T-A, both sides) — this digital subset, **pinned by slug**, is the channel-matched set that identifies B_type (§3.2); the founder physical **`flyer_huisaanhuis`** (tier T-A as primary acquisition, but **δ-loading and explicitly NOT part of the B_type-matched digital subset**, §2.6); **`buurtmailing`** (T-B postcode corrective rescue, UA2); **`marktplaats_hk`** (T-B substitute surface); **`fundainbusiness_fib`**, **`nvm_aankomend_aanbod`** (T-C substitute monitoring). **Tier T-A denotes the primary-acquisition tier (the digital buys + the founder flyer); the B_type-matched set is the digital subset {`meta`,`google_search`,`iamexpat`} named by slug, never "all T-A."** Acquisition is **digital-primary** by design; the founder door-to-door flyer is the physical mechanism handled as δ (§2.6, §3.6). ρ is reported per channel and the engine never silently pools rates across channels; the ignition gate (§6) aggregates *registration counts* across channels while the *rates* stay channel-separated. A change of channel defines a different estimand. **ρ is also conditional on the offered price** (the §6.8 fee): a campaign run at a different price is a different estimand, so price is held in the matched offer during identification (§3.2) and, if deliberately varied, enters the rate model as an elasticity term (§5.1, §6.8).

**2.4 Phase rule.** The engine never reports a figure under one rung with evidentiary weight earned only under another (the calibration-state machinery of §8 enforces this). Pre-launch, all rungs are prior-only. BE figures are a fourth, country-transported estimand, always tagged "transport-assumed" until a NL→BE harmonisation audit + BE pilots exist (§5.1, §9).

**2.5 Type-mean vs predictive — two B_type estimands (added v0.3).** B_type on the rate scale has two distinct readings that the harness cross-check (§7.1a) showed differ by 1.3–1.7× in interval width:
- **B_type type-mean** = E_u[expit(μ+u)], the population-mean rate of the matched postcode *type*, marginalising the between-PC4 random effect. **This is the transportable quantity and the object the ignition gate (§6) compares to ρ_lower.**
- **B_type predictive** = expit(μ+u) for one fresh draw of u, the rate of a *single as-yet-unseen postcode*, carrying a full τ of between-PC4 spread. This is the correct object for **single-PC4 rollout-risk** forecasting, not for the type-level gate.
The two share a mean but not a width. The engine reports both, explicitly labelled (`btype_mean`, `btype_rate`); conflating them is pre-registered as an estimand error, and §6's gate is fixed to the type-mean.

**2.6 Founder door-to-door flyer → δ (not a transportable channel).** The founder-led flyer in 1017/2584 is non-replicable founder presence: the founder cannot door-knock the control or expansion postcodes with the same local trust. It is therefore pre-registered as a component of the founder-presence uplift **δ** (the non-transportable rung), *not* as a channel contributing to B_type. (It is now carried in the engine as the `flyer_huisaanhuis` channel row — tier T-A primary-acquisition — purely for cost/steering bookkeeping; per this section it is δ-loading and is **not** in the B_type-matched digital subset of §2.3.) Consequently: (i) B_type is identified from the **channel-matched digital** response (§3.2), which is geo-targetable identically to founders and controls; (ii) the flyer is *not* channel-matched and *should not be* — matching it would be impossible and would contaminate B_type; (iii) the opt-in `reachable_fraction` (§3.6) caps unaddressed physical mail (the founder flyer and unaddressed buurtmailing), leaving B_type identification and digital acquisition unaffected. This keeps the three-rung ladder clean: digital → B_type (transports), founder flyer → δ (does not).

## 3. Design & data

**3.1 Unit of analysis.** The PC4 campaign; the PC6 wave within it. A campaign = a sustained flyer effort in one PC4. A wave = one PC6 flyered at one timestamp. Ignition = the §6 decision, not a data event.

**3.2 Designed-experiment elements (priced by the power calc, restated on the type-mean estimand).** The single highest-value design choice — confirmed by all four reviewers and priced by module 1 — is running **concurrent non-founder, demographically-matched control PC4s** to identify B_type, estimate τ², and seed the BE-transport template. The corrected (type-mean) power curve, 300 worlds/cell, true type-mean 0.876%:
- **(a) Control PC4s — the one real decision.** The first control collapses the B_type type-mean 90% credible interval from vacuous (16.0 pp, prior-only) to 2.31 pp; the second reaches 1.38 pp; 3–4 reach 1.08 / 0.87 pp (knee); beyond 4 the gains flatten (0.65 / 0.51 / 0.40 pp at 6 / 8 / 12). Coverage is **near-nominal (87–91%)** across the curve. The pre-registered options:
  - **B (floor):** 2 concurrent controls — the smallest design that makes B_type *identified* (1.38 pp) rather than prior-only.
  - **C/A (knee):** 3–4 concurrent controls — the precision sweet spot (1.08 / 0.87 pp), if budget and founder distribution-time allow.
  - **D (analytic-only):** 0 controls — B_type is prior-only (16.0 pp, vacuous) and the naive founder estimator is flat at +2.56 pp bias (≈3.9× the true rate); **explicitly tagged, never the engine's transportable number, and — per Module 3 — incapable of a credible two-sided ignite/abort call at all.**
  Controls are chosen by a **documented rule** (§3.3) and **randomised within the matched type**, and — critically — receive the **identical digital channel mix** (`meta`/`google_search`/`iamexpat`, geo-targeted to the control PC4) **and the identical price** (§6.8) as the founder PC4s. They are **not** flyered (the founder flyer is δ, §2.6). This keeps the founder-vs-control contrast a clean read of founder-presence under a matched offer, so B_type stays interpretable. **Decision (28 May 2026): option B — 2 concurrent controls.** Geo-targeted digital makes channel-matching cheap (no away-fieldwork); the expensive, unmatchable flyer stays founder-only. **Realized-balance caveat (per §3.3a-RESULT):** these option-B widths presuppose controls that type-match their founder. The executed draw achieves that for 2584 but **not for 1017** (a national outlier), so the simulated 1.38 pp is **optimistic for the Amsterdam-type** — there, B_type identification leans on the §5.1 MRP covariate adjustment (model extrapolation) rather than the matched contrast, and carries correspondingly wider, more assumption-dependent uncertainty. The §6.4 decision-flip criterion for the 1017-type is therefore evaluated on the MRP-adjusted posterior, not the matched-contrast width. **Extrapolation handling (1017):** because 1017 sits in a region with little national support on WOZ/density, the MRP *extrapolates* there rather than interpolates, and non-linear terms cannot fix this (curvature is unidentifiable where there is no data, and hopeless at G=4) — so it is handled, not hidden: (i) covariates are **capped at the data-support boundary** for the 1017-type prediction and any beyond-support prediction is reported with **explicitly widened extrapolation intervals, never a point estimate**; (ii) **reframe** — genuine Amsterdam-core expansion targets (rest of Amsterdam, other big-city cores) are *less* extreme than 1017, hence *interpolation*; the extrapolation risk is confined to a near-empty reference class ("more extreme than Amsterdam-Centrum"), so the **1017-type rate is explicitly not a primary steering number** — for Amsterdam-core rollout the engine weights 1017's *observed* post-launch rate (B_founder, δ bounded by the controls) above the model-extrapolated type-rate; (iii) a **§9 specification-sensitivity** (linear / capped / local-neighbour) whose spread is the honest uncertainty, resolving to WAIT if a decision flips across it.
- **(b) Randomised rescue holdout — optional.** A pre-specified subset of underperforming campaigns receives no rescue, giving a clean rescue control. Cheaper than (a) (it withholds effort rather than spends), but costs forgone registrations; retained as optional.
- **(c) Channel variation — demoted.** Deliberate channel variation to estimate stimulus-conditional contamination is parked (founder flyering is the single channel pre-launch); ρ is reported as flyer-channel-conditional per §2.3.
- **(d) Cluster count G.** Set by the power calc (§7), not convenience: G = 2 founders + the chosen control count. The power calc *is* the G-justification.

**3.3 Selection-on-outcome record (free; do regardless of B/C/D).** A pre-launch, dated document of how the founder PC4s were chosen — the candidate set considered, the scoring proxy, and the rule — so the selection bias that post-hoc data cannot remove is at least *bounded*. Without controls (option D) this record plus a reference-class discount is the only defence; with controls it tightens the bound. The SBC (§7.2) is the in-silico proof that this record cannot substitute for controls: δ stays non-recoverable without them. **Filed 28 May 2026.** The record establishes the founder PC4s were chosen by **founder residence for distribution access** (walkable door-to-door + existing neighbour ties) — no candidate-set search, no success-proxy, no data — so the winner's-curse selection-on-outcome term (the harness's σ_proxy = 0.30) is **near-zero in reality**, and δ is predominantly the founder-presence uplift (§2.6). The residual non-representativeness is **demographic** (the beachheads are affluent urban cores, §3.3a), handled by the §5.1 covariate adjustment, not a selection discount; the §7.2 δ-non-recoverability figure is therefore a conservative bound.

**3.3a Control PC4 selection / matching rule (pre-registered, reproducible, selection-free).** The 2 controls (option B) must each be a genuine demographic type-match to a founder PC4, so B_type is the transportable rate for "postcodes of this type." Procedure:
1. **Matching covariate vector X_match (9, as executed)** — CBS PC4 open data (`pc4_2023_v2`, ref. 1 Jan 2023; same family as the §5.1 MRP covariates and the per-campaign S1 CBS fact sheet, now stored in `pc4_cbs_facts`): % multi-family dwellings; % owner-occupied; % social-housing (corporatie); mean WOZ value (€1,000s); % benefit recipients; % age 25–45; % single-person households; address density (omgevingsadressendichtheid); and % foreign-born (the post-2022 origin/birth-country classification, the expat proxy — coarse, rounded to 10 pp). **Income is not populated at PC4 in this release**, so the planned standardised-household-income covariate is dropped and **WOZ value + benefit-recipient share stand in as the wealth/SES covariates** (documented per the drop-rule). Stratification is on CBS *stedelijkheid* (both founders are band 1). Covariates suppressed by CBS small-count privacy are dropped with documentation.
2. **Candidate pool** — all NL PC4s in the founder's urban-density band, **excluding** the 2 founder PC4s and any PC4 within **15 km (centroid distance, RD-New) of a founder** (avoids spatial-autocorrelation contamination, §7.3, campaign-spillover, and bowling-pin overlap; an initial coarse "4-digit-adjacent" rule was replaced because it admitted a 6.5 km neighbour).
3. **Distance** — z-standardise each covariate across the pool; compute **Mahalanobis** distance to the founder profile (Mahalanobis not Euclidean, because tenure/income/dwelling-type co-move).
4. **Caliper** — retain only candidates within a pre-registered caliper (Mahalanobis distance ≤ the closest-decile threshold), fixed before drawing.
5. **Selection-free draw** — from the within-caliper set, draw **1 control per founder at random under a pre-registered seed** (1 Amsterdam-type matched to 1017, 1 Den-Haag-type matched to 2584). Random-within-caliper is the step that prevents re-importing the founder's own outcome-based selection — it is what makes B_type selection-free.
6. **Balance acceptance / imbalance handling** — report standardised mean difference (SMD) per covariate between founder and drawn control. Where balance fails the |SMD| ≤ 0.25 target, **the imbalanced covariates are carried into the §5.1 MRP as explicit adjustment terms** — the SAP does **not** re-seed to chase balance, because re-drawing on observed balance would re-import outcome-based selection and break pre-registration. The single seeded draw stands; the SMD table is filed in the dated selection record (§3.3) and the failing covariates are named as adjustment terms.
7. **One-control-per-founder rationale** — with 2 founders spanning 2 cities/types, one matched control each keeps the founder-vs-control contrast within-type (cleanest read of δ). Pooling both controls against the combined founder type is the pre-registered fallback if a city has no within-caliper candidate.
8. **Treatment match** — drawn controls receive the identical digital channel mix (§2.6, §3.2) and are not flyered. Demographic match (this rule) + treatment match (channel) together identify B_type.

**3.3a-RESULT (executed 28 May 2026, seed 20260528, single draw).** On CBS `pc4_2023_v2` (4,070 PC4s), band-1 stratified, ≥15 km centroid separation:

| Founder | Control | City | Separation | Mahalanobis | max\|SMD\| |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1017 Amsterdam-Centrum | **3512** | Utrecht (centre) | 34.3 km | 3.59 | **1.92** |
| 2584 Den Haag-Statenkwartier | **2801** | Gouda | 30.0 km | 1.77 | **0.71** |

Full per-covariate SMD tables are in the dated selection record. **Finding:** neither control meets |SMD| ≤ 0.25, and **1017 misses badly** — Amsterdam-Centrum is a national statistical outlier (WOZ €815k, density 9,605, 40% foreign-born) with no close type-twin anywhere; the closest band-1 PC4 in another city sits ~1.6–1.9 SD away on wealth, density and diversity. 2584 is far more ordinary and gets a usable twin (Gouda, max|SMD| 0.71). **Consequence (the identification pivot): pure 1:1 matching cannot carry the identification here.** The matched controls are demoted to a *local sanity comparison*; the **§5.1 MRP covariate-adjusted estimator is the primary identifier of B_type**, with **WOZ, address density and % foreign-born as named adjustment covariates** (and % age 25–45 added for 2584). Operationally: **B_type transported from 1017 is the least trustworthy figure in the model** — its rate is entangled with the postcode's extremity — so the engine weights 2584's evidence and the adjusted estimate above 1017's raw rate when steering the rollout.
Caveats (→ §9): CBS small-count suppression; the origin/birth-country expat proxy is coarse (rounded to 10 pp); Mahalanobis assumes roughly elliptical covariates — a Gower-distance redraw is the pre-registered robustness alternative.

**3.4 Coverage measurement.** Reached-household counts recorded per wave (the engine's `flyer_distribution_log.count_distributed`), so ρ's denominator is observed, not assumed.

**3.5 Attribution.** The pre-specified wave→registration rule (attribution v0.1: same PC6, within 14 days, most-recent prior wave), with the window-sensitivity set {7, 14, 21 d} named now and reported regardless of outcome (§9).

**3.6 Mailbox opt-in regime and `reachable_fraction` (verified 28 May 2026).** Both founder cities run the **opt-in (Ja/Ja) system** for unaddressed advertising — Amsterdam since 2018, Den Haag since 1 July 2020. Unaddressed advertising is legal **only** at mailboxes displaying a Ja/Ja sticker; everything else is off-limits (€500/violation, enforced against advertiser and distributor). A preMercado flyer is commercial → bound by this. The flyer-reachable share `reachable_fraction` is therefore a minority of households — published evidence (Den Haag volume drop 9.8→~2.1 kg; Amsterdam opt-in studies) puts it at **~15–20%, conservative planning floor ~12%** (the §9 sensitivity set brackets this as 12 / 17 / 22%). This is *not committed as a point estimate*: it is a §9 sensitivity parameter, planned against the conservative end and **measured directly by the Wave-1 flyer-coverage log** (§3.4), self-correcting within weeks. Crucially, `reachable_fraction` caps **all unaddressed physical mail — the founder flyer/δ term (§2.6) and the unaddressed `buurtmailing` rescue channel alike** (both are commercial unaddressed mail, both bound by Ja/Ja); the digital channels (`meta`/`google_search`/`iamexpat`) reach residents online with no sticker constraint, so neither B_type identification nor primary acquisition is affected. **Rescue consequence:** an *unaddressed* buurtmailing inherits the same ~12–22% ceiling, so its effective reach and expected rescue lift must be ceiling-aware (the `rescue_playbook` BUURTMAIL expected-lift is over-stated if it ignores this) — which argues for escalating to the uncapped digital MKT_RESCUE rung sooner. An *addressed* buurtmailing (real names, bypassing the sticker) is a **distinct instrument** with a different cost profile and GDPR name-data requirements, tracked separately, not the same row. Legal exemptions noted for the complementary mix: addressed mail (*geadresseerd*, needs real names — note "aan de bewoners van" still counts as unaddressed) and huis-aan-huisbladen (≥30% editorial in Den Haag) bypass the rule.

## 4. Priors (set to the round-3 settled choices)

**4.1 Elicitation protocol.** Multi-expert SHELF elicitation is **demoted** (budget) to a single-source (founder) elicitation with *documented, pre-registered adjustments* for known biases (4.3c). Each prior carries its source and date (4.4).

**4.2 Prior distributions.**
- **τ² (between-PC4 variance):** a **penalised-complexity (PC) prior** on the SD scale (base model τ=0), reference-class-scaled to median ≈ 0.43 on the logit scale (the "top-decile 3× bottom-decile" reference, ln(3)/2.56). Inverse-gamma and half-Cauchy are explicitly rejected (pathological / too fat-tailed for sparse data). With no multi-PC4 data the prior *is* the posterior, so it is stress-tested (§9), not treated as weakly-informative. The SBC (§7.2) flags τ as the mildly-miscalibrated parameter (rank-mean ~55%, the classic variance-parameter SBC weak spot) — within the engine's tolerance, noted for the §9 stress test.
- **ρ (per segment, per rung, per side):** see 4.3 — a mixture, not a single Beta.
- **pro-share p_s, horizon-decay φ:** family and parameters fixed at lock; φ shape in the §9 sensitivity set.

**4.3 Triplet → distribution mapping (the round-3 settled rule).** Each (Conservative, Base, Aggressive) triplet is mapped to a **mixture of three Betas**, one per worldview, *not* a single Beta and *not* treated as 10/50/90 quantiles. Rules fixed at lock:
- Base = mode/centre of its component; Conservative/Aggressive centre their components as a wide envelope (5/95 or wider).
- Per-component effective sample size a+b small (≈2–5) — reported as "this prior ≈ N households" so its strength is visible.
- Pre-registered bias corrections applied *before* fixing parameters: widen for overconfidence (≈1.5–2×); let asymmetry stand; **recentre downward for founder optimism**.
- Mixture weights are themselves a reported output: posterior weights ("data moved us from 40% to 75% on Base") are the regime-learning signal. A single collapsed Beta is retained only for external display where one number is unavoidable.

**4.4 Prior provenance log.** Every prior tagged with source + elicitation date; readers can see assumption vs evidence.

## 5. Models & estimators

**5.1 Rate model (MRP) — primary identifier of B_type.** logit(ρ_i) = β·X_PC4 + founder_effect·F_i + segment random effect + PC4 random intercept ~ N(0, τ²), over public CBS PC4 covariates X_PC4 (housing-stock, age, owner/renter split, density; **income unavailable at PC4 → WOZ value + benefit share substitute**, per §3.3a); post-stratified to a target geography's covariate distribution for transport. **Per the §3.3a finding, this covariate-adjusted estimator — not the matched controls — is the primary identifier of B_type:** founder↔control imbalance (especially 1017's WOZ, address density and % foreign-born) is absorbed as adjustment terms here, and the matched controls serve as a supporting local comparison. **Attribution-leakage guard:** to stop last-touch attribution (§5.4) from crediting the digital channel when a flyer-prompted household converts via Google — which would silently load founder presence into B_type — the model includes a **founder-PC4 × channel interaction**, so flyer→digital spillover in the founder PC4s loads onto the founder term δ, not the shared digital coefficient; the controls' flyer-free digital response remains the clean B_type anchor. **Out-of-support predictions** (the 1017-type, §3.2) use boundary-capped covariates and report widened extrapolation intervals; non-linear covariate terms are deliberately *not* added, being unidentifiable without local data support (and hopeless at G=4). Partial pooling replaces pool/no-pool. **Price covariate (§6.8):** during B_type identification price is held constant across founders and controls (matched offer, §3.2), so it is *not* in the model; if per-campaign price is deliberately varied across the estimation set, a log-price elasticity term enters X_PC4 so the rate is read at a reference price rather than confounded by the offer. The τ² hyperprior is §4.2's PC prior. **Implementation note (revised v0.3):** the harness (module 1) fits a Gaussian-on-logit approximation; the binomial-exact cross-check (§7.1a) confirmed it reproduces the exact binomial posterior to ~7% on width and ~0.05 pp on the mean at the ~30-counts/PC4 design point. The **binomial-exact (NUTS / grid) refit is therefore a validated drop-in, retained for a future much-lower-count regime, not a required upgrade.** **Transport (NL→BE):** country enters as a partially-pooled effect with country-varying slopes on key covariates, post-stratified to the BE frame. The model *structure* transports; the covariate *definitions* do **not** — WOZ, CBS *stedelijkheid* and *corporatie* share have no clean BE equivalents (BE has *kadastraal inkomen*, a different social-housing structure, a different density construct), so BE requires a **covariate re-derivation** (an equivalent vector built from BE constructs), not a simple NL↔NIS-code mapping. The country effect carries the residual structural difference, and the **BE pilots (the control design re-run in BE) identify BE B_type fresh** rather than porting NL coefficients. BE figures stay "transport-assumed" until the re-derivation audit + 3–6 concurrent BE pilots exist; this is gated behind ID-verifier readiness and off the near-term path.

**5.2 Partial-campaign projection.** Non-homogeneous Poisson-Gamma with intensity tracking the pre-specified φ horizon-decay shape; predictive distribution for the final total.

**5.3 Ignition model — the per-side ρ_lower frontier (module 2).** ρ_lower is a **per-side pair** (ρ_lower^L, ρ_lower^S), each = max(unit-economics floor, liquidity floor):
- unit-econ floor ρ^econ_s = κ_s / (LTV_s · a_s) — registration high enough that per-household acquisition cost is repaid; under the working inputs L = 0.556%, S = 1.042% (seeking side binding);
- liquidity floor from the matching condition M = A·E_L^α·E_S^β with per-user match rate M/E_s ≥ m_s*.
Ignition is the **AND** of both sides clearing both floors → an ignition *region* in (ρ_L, ρ_S) space, not a scalar. The returns-to-scale α+β set the abort gate's character: α+β > 1 → tipping (sharp abort), α+β < 1 → soft floor. **A, α, β, m\* are pre-registered as assumption-grade unknowns**; the first campaigns' observed matches-per-user calibrate A directly. The gate uses the **unit-economics floors** (identified without A) as the conservative pre-calibration choice; the liquidity floor is the assumption-grade addition once A is measured. Mid-campaign monitoring against ρ_lower uses **anytime-valid** inference (confidence sequences / e-values) so sequential looks don't invalidate the test.

**5.4 Attribution model.** Deterministic last-touch (attribution v0.1) as baseline; time-decay / fractional split for overlapping waves as the pre-registered sensitivity (§9). Last-touch can credit digital for a *flyer-prompted* conversion in the founder PC4s; this leakage is contained by the §5.1 founder-PC4 × channel interaction (it loads to δ, not B_type) and probed by the {7/14/21 d} window sensitivity (§9).

**5.5 Rescue-effect identification.** Primary = regression-discontinuity on the deterministic rescue trigger (running variable = projected shortfall) and/or the randomised holdout (§3.2b); secondary = pooled, intervention-type-stratified hierarchical model. Pre-committed: single-campaign rescue effects are **never** reported as point estimates (the round-2 finding that the rescue estimator is a double-biased interrupted-time-series, not a DiD).

**5.6 Pooled-estimator fallback.** Cluster = PC4; household- vs cluster-weighting pre-specified (they target different estimands); wild-cluster bootstrap for the small G that the beachhead implies.

## 6. Decision rules

**6.1 Ignition trigger vs claim.** The internal operational ignition rule (an operating threshold) is kept separate from the external inferential ignition *claim* (ρ credibly above ρ_lower on both sides). The operating-characteristic curve of the operational rule is reported (module 3, §6.6).

**6.2 Ignition/abort as a per-side frontier with a subsidise-branch.** Evaluate (ρ_L, ρ_S) against the §5.3 frontier at 90% credibility on the **type-mean** posterior `btype_mean` (per §2.5; a side is credibly above if its q05 exceeds the floor, credibly below if its q95 is under it):
- both sides above → **ignited**;
- both sides below → **abort candidate** (sharp if α+β>1);
- **exactly one side below → subsidise the short side, do not abort** — a one-sided shortfall is a reallocation signal (the oversupplied side starves). This branch precedes the abort rule;
- otherwise → **wait** (insufficient evidence — the fail-safe default).

**6.3 Rescue trigger.** Fire when P(final total < target | data) exceeds a pre-specified threshold encoding the cost ratio of unnecessary-rescue vs missed-ignition.

**6.4 Estimand transitions as decision-flip conditions (not fixed counts).** B_founder → B_type is "identified" when the B_type **type-mean** posterior is tight enough that no §6 decision flips across its credible interval. The power calc (module 1) quantifies this on the type-mean estimand: 2 controls reach 1.38 pp width (option B), 3–4 reach the 1.08 / 0.87 pp knee (C/A). (Per the §3.2 realized-balance caveat, the 1017-type width is read off the MRP-adjusted posterior and is wider than the simulated 1.38 pp.) B_type → A likewise, gated on expansion-wave controls. State the decision-flip criterion explicitly at lock.

**6.5 Exit / expansion gate.** Liquidity self-sustenance (match/fill rate stable above m\* without paid injection) is the *binding* axis; penetration-vs-beachhead-SOM and saturation are supporting axes. Decouple "beachhead dominated" (a liquidity condition) from "national rate identifiable" (an estimability condition).

**6.6 Decision-level operating characteristics (module 3, type-mean gate).** The end-to-end pipeline (module 1 → module 2 → decision) scored against ground truth over the decision-relevant truth-band gives the pre-registered operating curve. Decisiveness (share of worlds the engine commits rather than waits) by control count k: **f=70% (≈t90):** 0 / 13 / 31 / 40 / 47% for k = 0…4; **f=100% (≈t180):** 0 / 17 / 38 / 44 / 51%. Accuracy when committed ≈79–85%. **Catastrophic mis-commits (ignite↔abort polarity flips) = 0.0% at every k and both horizons** — thin data resolves to WAIT, not danger (the fail-safe property). Three pre-registered reads: (i) option D (k=0) is 0% decisive — incapable of a credible two-sided call, not merely biased; (ii) controls and waiting are complementary levers (controls identify the type, waiting accumulates counts); (iii) the engine fails safe by construction, the single most important property for a self-funding founder. Absolute decisiveness levels depend on the truth-band design and the 90% bar; the ordering (k helps, waiting helps, catastrophic ≈ 0, D ≈ 0%) is the robust content.

**6.7 Ignition gate values and digital-budget re-baseline (verified live, 28 May 2026).** The ignition gate is the engine's **per-segment, per-city density thresholds**, *not* a uniform 25+25 — stored as `campaign_brief.ignition_target_listers/seekers` (with the per-segment breakdown in `campaign_brief.segment_targets`, derived from the density-gate figures) and evaluated by `v_campaign_feedback` against `agg_per_postcode.count`, the nightly aggregate of **verified-and-paid** users per PC4 per side. (The CCO report layer reads the same per-brief gate — confirmed and aligned in mig_045.) The gate is therefore denominated in **paid registrations**, which matches the per-registration CAC denominator exactly — there is no engaged/active haircut, because under UA1+UA3 a registration already *is* a verified, paying user. Verified values: The Hague semi-pro-landlords 6L/12S, garagebox-pro 5L/3S; Amsterdam expat-seekers 10L/8S, garagebox-pro 8L/5S, renters-randstad 10L/12S, semi-pro-landlords 10L/20S. Because acquisition is digital-primary (§2.3) and these gates are small, **ignition is digital-budget-bound, not flyer-reach-bound.** Costing the *entire* gate via paid digital at the live blended CAC (meta/google/iamexpat base ~€40/registration; range ~€15 early-tranche to ~€90 saturation): The Hague (26 regs) ≈ €0.4–2.3k; Amsterdam (83 regs) ≈ €1.2–7.5k; both founder PC4s ≈ **€1.6–9.8k, ~€4.4k at base CAC** — before the founder flyer (δ) and free community posting reduce the paid share. The opt-in `reachable_fraction` constraint (§3.6) does not gate ignition. **Caveat:** CACs are ML2 assumption-grade pre-launch figures, taken here as cost *per verified-paid registration* to match the gate's denominator (if ML2 measured CAC per signup rather than per paid registration, the per-paid-reg figure is modestly higher — a Wave-1 calibration item); calibrated by Wave-1; this re-baseline replaces the earlier flyer-only "25+25" framing.

**Self-funding / working-capital framing.** The ~€4.4k is a *gross* acquisition cost, not a required upfront float. Because every registration is paid (UA3), the acquired cohort bills as it arrives, and the gate is pro-weighted. At the §6.8 placeholder prices the per-*registration* monthly revenue is ~€20 for a garagebox-pro user (≈ **2-month CAC payback** at €40 CAC — note the €480/yr ARPU is per both-sided SAM-unit, i.e. two registrations, so €240/yr ≈ €20/mo *per registration*), ~€16 for a semi-pro lister (~2.5-month payback), and ~€2 for the consumer seeker sides (~20-month payback). The pro registrations alone (garagebox-pro + semi-pro listers, ≈ 37 of the 109 gate registrations) bill on the order of **€8k in year one** — roughly twice the €4.4k gross — so in aggregate ignition is self-funding, with the pro surplus cross-funding the slow-payback consumer sides. The binding constraint is therefore **working capital for the CAC-payback lead-lag** (CAC due at acquisition, billings arriving over the following months), largest at campaign start and shrinking as recurring billings stack up — not the €4.4k as a lump sum. This makes per-campaign price (§6.8) a direct lever on that float: cutting the short-side price fills the gate faster (more paid registrations sooner) at the cost of lower ARPU and slower payback.

**6.8 Pricing model and per-campaign price (placeholders + identification rule).** Revenue is a **recurring monthly subscription** (non-refundable, 1-month minimum); there is no separate one-time registration fee. The fee primitives are placeholders pending the verifier price + beta/user-interview calibration: **consumer €2/mo, pro €20/mo** (a 900% / 10× relation — HIGH confidence on the *ratio*, LOW on the *absolute levels*), set city-level not segment-level at this layer. A segment's annual ARPU is `((1−ps)·consumer + ps·pro) × 12 × side_mult`, where ps is the segment pro-share and side_mult is 2 for both-sided segments (e.g. garagebox, per UA3) and 1 otherwise; base-scenario examples: consumer-seeker €24, semi-pro (ps 0.8) €196.80, garagebox-pro (ps 1, both-sided) €480/SAM-unit (= two registrations at €240 each). These are recorded as assumption-grade and stress-tested in §9.

**Per-campaign price** is pre-registered as an *override* capability: the data model already expresses it (`figures` carries `atomic_network_id`, so a campaign-scoped price row can override the city/global default via a resolution cascade — a computation change, not a schema change; the resolution rule itself is the one remaining engine build item). The identification rule is the load-bearing constraint: **during B_type identification, price is held constant across a founder PC4 and its matched control** (a matched offer, exactly like the channel match of §3.2); otherwise price confounds the rate and must enter the MRP as a log-price elasticity term (§5.1). Outside the identification window price is free, with three pre-registered roles: (i) a **rescue rung** below MKT_RESCUE on the §5.5 ladder — cut the starved short side's price to fill its gate faster (the §6.7 working-capital lever); (ii) **WTP capture on expansion**, anchored to a transparent rule (e.g. a WOZ band from the §3.3a fact sheet) rather than ad hoc, to avoid the fairness risk of arbitrary adjacent-postcode price gaps; (iii) a **deliberately pre-registered price-test arm** if elasticity is to be learned (the control design supports it), never uncontrolled per-campaign drift. Any price variation used for estimation is reported in §9.

## 7. Validation plan

**7.1 Generative simulation harness (BUILT, end-to-end).** The digital twin is complete across five modules: module 1 (`engine_harness.py`) generates worlds with known ground-truth (including explicit selection-on-outcome), fits the hierarchical model, and the power sweep (`run_power.py`, `run_typemean_widths.py`) fixed the §3.2 control-count cost-benefit and the §6.4 decision-flip widths on the type-mean estimand. Module 2 (`rho_lower.py`) builds the per-side ignition frontier and gate-calibration. Module 3 (`decision_pipeline.py`) composes 1+2 into the scored ignite/subsidise/abort/wait decision and produced the §6.6 operating characteristics. Module 4 (`binom_exact_check.py`, `sbc.py`) is the validation layer (§7.1a, §7.2). Module 5 (`sequential_rescue.py`) adds anytime-valid sequential monitoring and the rescue-RDD (§7.4).

**7.1a Binomial-exact cross-check (DONE).** `binom_exact_check.py` recomputes the B_type posterior under the exact binomial likelihood (24-node Gauss–Hermite marginalisation of the per-PC4 random effect on a dense (μ, δ, τ) grid; no sampler). Result: once the estimand is aligned (§2.5), the fast fit matches the exact type-mean posterior to ~7% on width and ~0.05 pp on the mean, the 7% running slightly over-confident. The 1.3–1.7× apparent gap was the predictive-vs-type-mean estimand difference, not approximation error. **Pre-registered conclusion: the binomial-exact refit is not required at the design point; it is the validated drop-in for a future low-count regime.**

**7.2 Simulation-based calibration (SBC) — the success criterion (DONE).** `sbc.py`, two designs, ranks of truth within 999 posterior draws. SBC-A (clean, all-random postcodes): type-mean and τ ranks show ECDF max-deviation from uniform ≈ 0.07–0.08 vs a 95% KS band of 0.043 — detectable at n=1000 but small in absolute terms (worst-case coverage error ~7–8 pp; type-mean slightly over-confident, τ slightly low-biased). SBC-B (2 selection-on-outcome founders + 4 controls): B_type rank-mean ~45% (controls anchor it, ~calibrated) but **δ rank-mean 155/999 → the founder uplift is non-recoverable** when selection is unmodelled — the in-silico proof that controls are non-negotiable. **Pre-registered criterion at lock:** the fast fit's calibration is accepted to within ~7–8 pp worst-case coverage error over the operating regime, justified by (i) the 90% credibility bar, (ii) the §6.6 fail-safe property, and (iii) the ±20% tolerances the engine already adopts. (This *replaces* the v0.2 note of "conservative 97–99% coverage" — that figure was the predictive interval scored against a type-mean truth; on the correct estimand coverage is near-nominal, §3.2.)

**7.3 Pre-specified failure-mode battery.** founder-uplift × attribution-leakage interaction; rescue regression-to-mean (Ashenfelter); selection-on-outcome / winner's-curse (now demonstrated, §7.2); estimand-confusion (A-estimator in the B phase; **and predictive-vs-type-mean per §2.5**); correlated coverage; overdispersion (NegBin vs Poisson); spatial autocorrelation across adjacent simultaneous campaigns; Simpson's paradox on pooling; φ mis-specification; matching mis-specification (α+β regime) for ρ_lower.

**7.4 Sequential monitoring + rescue-RDD (DONE — module 5, `sequential_rescue.py`).** Both extensions are built and exercised. **Sequential (§5.3):** over 12 weekly looks against the seeking floor, a naive repeated-90%-interval rule false-commits **30.9%** under the null (3× nominal) — the peeking problem — while an **anytime-valid mSPRT e-process holds it to ~0%** (Ville) and still detects true departures with 0% wrong-direction. Pre-registered rule: weekly monitoring is permitted only under the anytime-valid e-process; naive repeated looks are prohibited. **Rescue (§5.5):** sharp RDD recovers the true local rescue effect ~unbiased (bias ≤0.06 across n), SE 1.07→0.29 from 100→1,200 campaigns; the naive rescued-vs-control comparison is biased ~−1.1 (≈50% understatement) by quality confounding. Pre-registered rule: rescue effects are a post-scale estimand (RDD or the §3.2b holdout), needing ~600–1,200 campaigns near the cutoff, and are never single-campaign point estimates. **The harness is now complete across all five modules; no analytical item remains.**

## 8. Calibration governance

**8.1 Four-layer uncertainty separation (the organising principle).** Every reported interval separates the four uncertainty sources the engine must not conflate: **sampling** (how noisy is this observed rate), **transport** (will another postcode behave similarly — τ²; note the type-mean vs predictive distinction of §2.5 is exactly the choice of whether to *fold transport in* or report it separately), **selection** (did we pick favourable launch areas — the founder rung), **structural** (does the mechanism even hold in another market/country). Collapsing these into one number is the failure the engine exists to avoid.

**8.2 Per-number calibration state.** Every figure tagged {prior-only | data-supported | data-refuted} × {A | B_type | B_founder | BE-transport}.

**8.3 Evidence colour-coding** for external artifacts; an A-figure may never be shown with confidence earned only under B; a BE figure never with NL evidentiary weight.

**8.4 Terminology discipline.** "Expected" reserved for posterior expectations; "prior-implied / planned / design target" for prior-only quantities; internal-consistency checks (e.g. the demand-sum invariant) are "wiring checks," never "validation." Rate figures specify type-mean vs predictive (§2.5) wherever the distinction affects a width.
